What it does

  • Five document types: invoice, quote, estimate, receipt and credit note
  • Tax set up your way: none, VAT, GST (split or combined), IGST, sales tax or a custom tax
  • Five designs, with your own accent colour
  • A client list and drafts saved on this device, which you can export, import or erase
  • The PDF has real text, so it can be searched and copied

How it works

  1. Open Business docs and choose the type of document.
  2. Fill in your details, the client and the lines, and pick a design.
  3. Check the totals, then download the PDF.

Your files stay on this device

The files are read and written by your browser on this device. Nothing is uploaded. Your client list and drafts are saved in this browser only.

Questions

Short, honest answers.

More on the privacy and install page.

Is it accounting software?

No. It makes documents. It does not track payments, send reminders or file taxes.

Does it work out tax for me?

It adds up the tax type and rates you choose. It does not know your obligations, so check the totals and the tax wording with your adviser.

Where is my client list kept?

In this browser on this device. You can export it as a file, import it elsewhere, or erase it.

Can I turn a PDF into a spreadsheet instead?

Yes, the same panel has a tool for extracting tables from a PDF.
