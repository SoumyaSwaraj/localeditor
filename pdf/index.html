Your files stay on this device

PDFs are read and written by your browser using open-source libraries that ship with the site. Your documents are not uploaded, so a contract, a payslip or a signed form stays on the device you opened it on.

Questions

Short, honest answers.

More on the privacy and install page.

Are my PDFs uploaded anywhere?

No. Opening, editing and saving all happen in this browser. You can switch off your connection after the page loads and keep working.

Can I sign a PDF on my phone?

Yes. Draw your signature with a finger, place it, and save the PDF. The signature is kept only on this device if you choose to save it.

Can it open Word or Excel files?

Convert to PDF turns Word, Excel, PowerPoint, images and text files into a PDF on this device. Complex layouts may not match the original exactly.
