Checked here, kept here

None of these tools send a file anywhere. Reading a photo's camera data, listing what is stored and clearing it are all done by your browser. The storage manager shows the app's own caches and saved work, so you can delete any of it in one place.

Questions

Short, honest answers.

More on the privacy and install page.

What hidden data can a photo carry?

Phone photos often include the date and time, the camera or phone model, and sometimes the GPS location where the photo was taken. The metadata tool shows what is there.

What does the app store on my device?

Saved designs and projects, the app itself for offline use, and any fonts, graphics or models you chose to download. The storage manager lists each with its size and a clear button.

Does using these tools make my files private by law?

No. These tools help you see and remove information, but they are not legal advice and do not make a process compliant with any regulation. Check your own organisation's rules.
