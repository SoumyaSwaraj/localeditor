Make a screenshot or some code worth posting.

Paste a screenshot, or up to 40 lines of code, and get an image in a plain window or device frame on a backdrop, ready to share. The result is made of ordinary layers, so you can keep editing it.

What it does

  • Paste, drop or pick a screenshot, or switch to code, which is coloured for common languages such as JavaScript, Python, HTML and CSS
  • Browser, desktop, phone or tablet frames, drawn as plain shapes
  • Five backdrop styles, backdrop colours of your own, and choices for space, corners and shadow
  • An optional headline above the window
  • Square, 4:5, 16:9 and story sizes, and Edit details to remake it later

How it works

  1. Open the beautifier and paste your screenshot (Ctrl+V, or long-press and Paste on a phone), or switch to Code and paste it.
  2. Choose a frame, a backdrop, a size and a shadow. The preview updates as you go.
  3. Tap Make it, then edit any part or download.

Your files stay on this device

What you type and the photos you add stay on this device. The preview and the finished design are drawn by your browser, and nothing is uploaded. Code is coloured by a small highlighter that ships with the site.

Questions

Short, honest answers.

More on the privacy and install page.

Does it use a real browser or phone frame?

No. The frames are generic: neutral dots, a plain address bar, an even bezel and a round camera dot. They are drawn from shapes, so you can recolour them.

How much code can I paste?

Up to 40 lines and 2,000 characters. The language can be detected, or you can choose it.

Does it keep my screenshot’s shape?

The image takes the size you choose (square, 4:5, 16:9 or story) and the screenshot is fitted inside it. If you need another shape, resize the page after making it.

Can I edit the result?

Yes. A maker produces ordinary layers. Tap any part to change it, move it or replace a photo, or use Edit details to reopen the form and remake the page.
