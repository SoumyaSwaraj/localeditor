    How to reorder and delete PDF pages

    1. Drop your PDF into the box or tap to choose it; a thumbnail of every page appears.
    2. Drag pages into a new order or use the arrow buttons, and delete, duplicate, rotate or insert blank pages as needed.
    3. Click Save PDF to download the reorganized document.

    What is Organize PDF?

    Organize PDF is a page manager that lets you change the order and content of pages in a PDF. Thumbnails are rendered with Mozilla’s pdf.js, and the new file is assembled with pdf-lib’s copyPages, which copies each original page object as-is.

    Because pages are copied rather than re-rendered, the export is lossless: text stays selectable, images keep their quality, and file size stays close to the original. Rotation is stored in the page’s /Rotate setting in 90° steps; blank pages match the size of the neighboring page.

    The tool runs entirely in your browser, so the PDF is never uploaded, and it works offline. Drag with a mouse anywhere on a page card, or on touch screens use the grip handle so normal scrolling keeps working. Password-protected PDFs must be unlocked first.

    Frequently asked questions

    How do I rearrange pages in a PDF?

    Drop your PDF into this tool, then drag the page thumbnails into the order you want, or use the left and right arrow buttons on each page. Click Save PDF to download the result.

    How do I delete pages from a PDF?

    Click the trash icon on any page you want to remove. Deleted pages are left out when you save, and Reset restores the original order if you change your mind.

    Does reorganizing reduce PDF quality?

    No. Pages are copied directly from the original file without re-rendering, so text, vector graphics and images keep their original quality.

    Can I reorder pages on my phone?

    Yes. Press and drag the grip handle on a page to move it on touch screens, or tap the arrow buttons to move a page one position at a time.

    Are my files uploaded?

    No. All processing happens locally in your browser. Your PDF never leaves your device and the tool works without an internet connection.
