How to split a PDF

  1. Drop a PDF file into the box, or tap to choose one.
  2. Pick a mode: extract page ranges, split every page, or split every N pages. Click thumbnails to select pages.
  3. Press Split PDF, then download each output file or all of them.

What is a PDF splitter?

A PDF splitter creates new PDF files from selected pages of an existing PDF. This tool offers three modes: extract ranges such as "1-3,5,8-10" into one PDF (or one file per range), split every page into its own PDF, or split into chunks of N pages.

Pages are copied losslessly with pdf-lib, so text stays selectable and images keep full resolution. Thumbnails are rendered locally with pdf.js. Your PDF never leaves your device and the tool works offline after the first visit.

Frequently asked questions

How do I extract specific pages from a PDF?

Choose Extract ranges and type pages like 1-3,5,8-10, or click page thumbnails to select them. Press Split PDF to get a new PDF containing only those pages.

Can I split a PDF into single pages?

Yes. Choose Every page and each page becomes its own PDF file. Download them one by one or all at once.

Is my PDF uploaded anywhere?

No. Splitting happens in your browser with JavaScript. The file never leaves your device and the tool also works offline.

Does splitting reduce quality?

No. Pages are copied without re-rendering, so text, fonts and images remain identical to the original PDF.

Is there a page or file size limit?

There is no fixed limit. Very large PDFs depend on your device memory; desktop browsers comfortably handle files with hundreds of pages.
