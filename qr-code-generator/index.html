What is QR Code Generator?

A QR code (Quick Response code) is a two-dimensional barcode that stores up to about 2,953 bytes of data in a grid of black and white modules. Phone cameras read it instantly and open the link, join the Wi-Fi network or save the contact it encodes.

This generator encodes your data with the open-source qrcode-generator library, automatically picking the smallest QR version (1–40) for your content. Wi-Fi codes use the standard WIFI:T:WPA;S:name;P:password;; format, and contacts use vCard 3.0.

Error correction lets a code survive damage: L recovers about 7% of data, M 15%, Q 25% and H 30%. The QR code is built entirely in your browser, so Wi-Fi passwords and contact details are never uploaded. Generated codes are static and never expire.

Frequently asked questions

Do these QR codes expire?

No. They are static QR codes that store your data directly in the pattern, so they work forever and do not depend on any server or tracking service.

How do I make a Wi-Fi QR code?

Choose the Wi-Fi type, enter the network name (SSID), password and security type. Guests can scan it with their phone camera to join without typing the password.

Should I download PNG or SVG?

Use PNG for websites, documents and messaging. Use SVG for print, signage and logos because it is a vector format that scales to any size without blurring.

Which error correction level should I use?

M (15%) is a good default. Choose Q or H if the code will be printed small, placed on rough surfaces or partly covered, at the cost of a denser pattern.

Is my data private?

Yes. The QR code is generated on your device with JavaScript. Nothing you type, including Wi-Fi passwords, is sent to a server.

Why won't my QR code scan?

Keep strong contrast with a dark foreground on a light background, leave a quiet-zone margin of at least 4 modules, and avoid printing the code too small for its amount of data.
