What it does

  • Word (.docx), Excel (.xlsx) and PowerPoint (.pptx)
  • JPG, PNG and WebP images, one per page or combined
  • Markdown, HTML and plain text
  • Send the result straight to the PDF studio to sign or merge

How it works

  1. Open Convert to PDF and drop in your files.
  2. Check the preview and the page settings.
  3. Save the PDF, or open it in the PDF studio.

Your files stay on this device

Conversion runs in your browser with libraries that ship with the site. The document is not uploaded to a conversion service.

Questions

Short, honest answers.

More on the privacy and install page.

Will the PDF look exactly like the original?

Simple documents convert closely. Complex layouts, unusual fonts and some spreadsheet features may look different, so check the preview.

Does it handle old .doc or .xls files?

The converter reads the current .docx, .xlsx and .pptx formats. Save older files in the newer format first.
