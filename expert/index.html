Expert mode

Studio-level editing, in a browser tab.

Switch the photo studio to Expert mode for a develop panel with collapsible sections, a tool strip, layers with masks and blend modes, scopes and a before and after view. More than 110 effects are adjustable settings, so nothing is baked into your photo.

Edited on this device, not on a server

Expert mode runs on your device, using your graphics hardware, and photos are never uploaded. A few features use small models that download from this site once: a depth map (about 16 MB), face points (about 4 MB) and an optional camera RAW decoder (about 1 MB). They run on your device and are listed in the storage manager.

Questions

Short, honest answers.

More on the privacy and install page.

How do I get into Expert mode, and out of it?

Use the Simple and Expert switch at the top of the studio, or open the studio with ?mode=expert. The choice is remembered on this device. Switching never changes your design.

Will my design open in the simple studio?

Yes. Both draw the same design. In the simple studio, layers edited in Expert carry an Edited in Expert marker, and one tap takes you back.

What does it need?

A browser with WebGL 2, which most current phones and computers have. Expert mode is designed for a computer or tablet and opens on a phone with a compact layout. The Device check page shows what your browser supports.

Is it a copy of another editor?

No. It follows conventions that professionals know, such as a develop panel, a tool strip, masks and scopes, in LocalEditor’s own design. It is a real but smaller set of tools, and it covers colour, light, retouching and product work.
