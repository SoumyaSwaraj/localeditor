Film and creative effects

Make a photo look like film, with effects you can adjust.

Expert mode adds dozens of effects: film grain and halation, bloom and glow, light leaks, dust and scratches, lens blur and tilt-shift, sharpening, and stylised looks. Each one is a setting you can adjust, reorder or turn down, not a change baked into the photo.

What it does

  • Film looks: grain, halation, fade, cross process, light leaks, dust and scratches
  • Glow and light: bloom, glow, soft focus, haze, sun rays and lens flare
  • Blur: lens blur, tilt-shift, iris, motion, radial and surface blur
  • Detail: sharpen, clarity, texture, dehaze and noise reduction
  • Stylise: duotone, halftone, pixelate, glitch, oil paint and sketch

How it works

  1. Open the studio in Expert mode and add a photo.
  2. Choose Add effect and search by name, for example grain, bloom or blur.
  3. Adjust the sliders, stack as many effects as you like, then download.

Your files stay on this device

Photos are edited by your browser on this device, using your graphics hardware. Nothing is uploaded, there is no account, and nothing is added to what you export.

Questions

Short, honest answers.

More on the privacy and install page.

How do I make a photo look like film?

Stack Film grain, Halation and Fade, then add a light leak or a vignette if you like. Each has its own sliders and amount, so you can keep the look subtle.

Can I blur the background of a photo?

Yes. Lens blur and the other blur effects can be limited to part of the picture with a mask. Depth blur blurs by distance instead, and needs a depth map: choose the Light tool and its Depth map button to make one on your device, after a one-off download of about 16 MB from this site. A depth map is an estimate, so edges such as hair can need touching up with a mask.

Will the export match what I see?

Yes. The export uses the same settings as the preview and is drawn from your original photo, not from the preview.

Do effects slow my device down?

Heavy effects switch to a lighter preview while you drag a slider and sharpen when you let go. A long stack on an older phone can still be slow, so switch effects off to find the heavy one.
