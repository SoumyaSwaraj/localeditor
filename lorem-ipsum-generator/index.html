What is Lorem Ipsum Generator?

A Lorem Ipsum generator produces dummy Latin-like text for mockups, templates and typesetting. The classic passage begins “Lorem ipsum dolor sit amet, consectetur adipiscing elit” and comes from Cicero’s De finibus bonorum et malorum (45 BC).

This generator picks words at random from a built-in list of about 180 Lorem Ipsum words. Sentences contain 6 to 16 words and paragraphs 4 to 7 sentences, so the text has a natural rhythm. You can output up to 1,000 paragraphs, sentences or words, as plain text or wrapped in HTML <p> tags.

All text is generated locally in your browser with no network requests, so it works offline.

Frequently asked questions

What is Lorem Ipsum?

Lorem Ipsum is scrambled Latin placeholder text used in design and publishing since the 1500s. It is derived from Cicero's 45 BC work De finibus bonorum et malorum and lets people judge a layout without being distracted by readable content.

How many paragraphs, sentences or words can I generate?

You can generate from 1 to 1,000 paragraphs, sentences or words at a time. Each sentence has 6 to 16 words and each paragraph has 4 to 7 sentences.

Can I get Lorem Ipsum as HTML?

Yes. Choose the HTML <p> format and every paragraph is wrapped in <p> tags, ready to paste into a web page, CMS or code editor.

Does the text always start with Lorem ipsum dolor sit amet?

Only if you want it to. The start option is on by default; untick it to get fully random placeholder text.

Is the generator free and offline?

Yes. The text is generated in your browser from a built-in Latin word list, so it is free, needs no account and works without an internet connection.
