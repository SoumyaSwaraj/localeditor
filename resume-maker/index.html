CV maker

Your CV on one page, clear and easy to read.

Fill in your name, contact details, a short summary, your experience, education and skills. The maker sets them on one page in a quiet, readable layout, and shrinks the text together when there is a lot of it.

What it does

  • One page on A4 or US Letter in four restrained styles: classic, side column, compact and accent
  • Name, job title, contact details, a summary, up to five jobs, three courses and ten skills
  • The form limits how long each part can be and the text shrinks together, so a full form still fits on the page
  • An optional photo, and an accent colour of your own
  • Exports as a PDF at the real paper size, or as an image

How it works

  1. Fill in your details, your experience with the newest job first, your education and your skills.
  2. Choose a style and a paper size, then tap Make it. The CV opens in the editor.
  3. Read it through, change any wording on the page, then download it as a PDF.

Your files stay on this device

What you type, the photos you add and any list you bring stay on this device. The design, the QR codes and the exported files are made by your browser, and nothing is uploaded.

Questions

Short, honest answers.

More on the privacy and install page.

Is the PDF text selectable?

No. The page is exported as an image inside the PDF at print resolution, so it looks the same everywhere, but the text cannot be selected or read by software that scans CVs for keywords. If an employer asks for a text CV, send a word-processor file as well.

How much fits?

One page: up to five jobs, three courses and ten skills. The form limits how long each part can be, and the text shrinks together to fit, to about three quarters of its size at most, so even a full form stays on one page.

Can I make these from a spreadsheet?

No. A CV is a single document, so this maker has no list button.

Is my CV stored anywhere?

Only on this device, as a saved design in your browser, until you delete it. Nothing is uploaded.

Can I change the result?

Yes. It opens in the editor as an ordinary design. Tap any part to change it, or use Edit details to reopen the form and remake it in one undo step. If a design is already open, the new pages are added after the page you are on.
