Your files stay on this device

Photos are opened, edited and saved by your browser on this device. They are not sent to a server, so there is nothing for us to keep, scan or lose. The studio only reaches the internet when you ask it to, for example to search a stock photo library.

Questions

Short, honest answers.

More on the privacy and install page.

Do I need an account?

No. Open the studio and start. Your designs are saved in this browser, and you can save a project file to keep or move them.

Does it work on a phone?

Yes. The studio is designed for phones first, with a bottom sheet for tools and large touch targets. Very large photos are easier on a computer.

Is there a watermark on exports?

No. Exports are your own files with nothing added.
