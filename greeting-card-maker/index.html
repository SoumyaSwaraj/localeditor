A card to fold, with your words inside.

What it does

  • Thank you, birthday, congratulations and season's greetings, or your own words on the front
  • A card that folds to A5 or A6, or a flat 5 × 7 in card, in six styles
  • Two pages: the outside (front and back) and the inside with Dear, your message and who it is from
  • A dashed fold line you can switch off, and an optional photo on the front
  • One card per person from a list. Exports as a PDF at the real paper size, or as images

How it works

  1. Choose the occasion and write the message.
  2. Choose a style and a size, then tap Make it. The outside and the inside open in the editor as two pages.
  3. Print the outside, turn the sheet over and print the inside on the back, then fold along the line.

Your files stay on this device

What you type, the photos you add and any list you bring stay on this device. The design, the QR codes and the exported files are made by your browser, and nothing is uploaded.

How do I print a folded card?

The card that folds to A5 is A4 turned sideways, and the one that folds to A6 is A5 turned sideways. Print the first page, put the sheet back in and print the second page on the other side, flipping on the short edge, at 100 percent. The front is on the right half of the first page and the message on the right half of the second, so they sit correctly once folded.

Can I make one for each person from a spreadsheet?

Yes. Tap Make one for each row of a list. Columns called name and note fill the inside of each card, and the button opens Create many on the inside page for you. Save one PDF or a zip of images. The front is the same on every card, so print it as many times as you have rows.

Will colour print to the edge?

Only on a printer that prints without borders. Most home printers leave a few millimetres white around the sheet. The Simple and Script styles look right with that edge.

What paper should I use?

Card of about 200 to 250 grams per square metre folds well and takes ink on both sides. Score the fold with a ruler and a blunt edge first for a clean crease.

Can I change the result?

Yes. It opens in the editor as an ordinary design. Tap any part to change it, or use Edit details to reopen the form and remake it in one undo step. If a design is already open, the new pages are added after the page you are on.
