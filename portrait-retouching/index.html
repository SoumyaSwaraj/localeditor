Portrait retouching

Retouch portraits without losing the skin.

Expert mode has a retouching set for portraits: heal and clone for spots, frequency separation that smooths tone and keeps texture, skin and face tools, red-eye removal, and dodge and burn painted with a brush. The original photo is never changed.

What it does

  • Heal and clone brushes to remove spots, dust and small distractions
  • Frequency separation and detail bands: smooth colour and tone while keeping skin texture
  • Skin smoothing, blemishes and shine, which find skin by colour, or by face once faces are found
  • Eyes, under-eyes, teeth, lips and face contour, which work on a face the app has found
  • Dodge and burn with a brush to shape light and shadow, and red-eye removal

How it works

  1. Open the studio in Expert mode and add a portrait.
  2. Use the Heal or Clone tool for spots. For skin, open Retouch in the panel and add Portrait retouch or Frequency separation.
  3. For eyes, teeth and lips, run Find faces in the Liquify tool first. Check with the before and after view, then download.

Your files stay on this device

Photos are edited by your browser on this device, using your graphics hardware. The face map the skin tools use comes from a small model that downloads from this site once, runs on your device and is listed in the storage manager. Nothing you edit is sent anywhere.

Questions

Short, honest answers.

More on the privacy and install page.

Will it make skin look fake?

Strong smoothing can. Every tool has an amount slider, and frequency separation keeps the fine texture, so modest settings look natural. Use the before and after view to check.

Does it find every face?

No. Face finding is an estimate, and a small, turned or covered face can be missed. A mask lets you limit an effect to where you want it.

Does it change my original photo?

No. Healing, cloning and dodge and burn are stored as a layer over the untouched photo, and the other tools are settings.

Can I paint on a phone?

Yes, with one finger to paint and two to zoom, but Expert mode is designed for a larger screen.
