What it does

  • Use any photo as the background, or detach it again
  • Crop, pan, zoom and flip directly on the canvas
  • Fill, fit, stretch or tile
  • Blur and dim so text on top stays readable
  • Solid colours, gradients, patterns and textures as well

How it works

  1. Open Background in the studio and pick a photo, colour or gradient.
  2. Drag to position it, then set fit, blur and dim.
  3. Add your text or photos on top and download.

Your files stay on this device

The background photo stays on this device. Every adjustment is saved as settings in your design, so you can change it later without re-uploading anything, because nothing was uploaded.

Questions

Short, honest answers.

More on the privacy and install page.

Does blurring change my original photo?

No. Blur, dim and crop are settings on the background, so you can undo or change them at any time.

Can it remove the background behind a person?

This page is about the background layer of a design. To cut a person or a product out of a photo, use the background remover.
