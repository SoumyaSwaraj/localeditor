What it does

  • A4 and US Letter in five styles, with your logo or name, address, phone, email and website in the header and footer
  • The body is left clear, with a light sample paragraph to type over
  • A matching compliment slip and envelope front as extra pages
  • An optional faint watermark, made from your logo when you add one
  • Exports as a PDF at the real paper size or as images, and one addressed letter per row of a list

How it works

  1. Fill in your company details and add a logo if you have one.
  2. Choose a style and a paper size, switch on the slip and the envelope if you want them, then tap Make it.
  3. Type your letter over the sample paragraph in the editor, then download a PDF or print.

Your files stay on this device

What you type, the photos you add and any list you bring stay on this device. The design, the QR codes and the exported files are made by your browser, and nothing is uploaded.

Questions

Short, honest answers.

More on the privacy and install page.

Can I address letters to a whole list from a spreadsheet?

Yes. Tap Make one for each row of a list. Columns called name and address fill the recipient block and the greeting, and you get one PDF with a page per letter, or a zip of images. The body text is the same on every letter. Create many fills the page you are looking at, so open the envelope page and run it again to address the envelopes.

Can I use it in a word processor?

Export the page as a PDF or PNG and set it as the page background in your word processor, or print a stack of letterheads and feed them through the printer again. The maker itself is for the design and for short letters typed on the page.

Which sizes are the slip and the envelope?

With A4, the slip is 210 × 99 mm and the envelope front is DL, 220 × 110 mm. With US Letter, the slip is 8.5 × 3.67 in and the envelope is a No. 10, 9.5 × 4.125 in.

Will the colour band print to the edge?

Only on a printer that prints without borders, or at a print shop. Most home printers leave a few millimetres white. The Classic and Minimal styles keep everything inside the margins.

Can I change the result?

Yes. It opens in the editor as an ordinary design. Tap any part to change it, or use Edit details to reopen the form and remake it in one undo step. If a design is already open, the new pages are added after the page you are on.
