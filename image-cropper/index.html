How to crop an image

  1. Drop an image onto the box or tap to choose one.
  2. Drag the crop box or its handles, pick an aspect ratio like 1:1 or 16:9, or type exact X, Y, width and height in pixels.
  3. Choose the output format and click Crop & download.

What is an Image Cropper?

An image cropper removes the outer parts of a picture to improve framing or to match a required shape. This tool shows your image with a movable, resizable crop box: drag inside it to move, drag any of the eight handles to resize, or focus the box and use the arrow keys for pixel-precise nudges (Shift moves 10 px).

Aspect presets lock the box to Free, 1:1 (square, profile pictures), 4:3, 16:9 (YouTube, widescreen) or 9:16 (Stories, Reels, TikTok). Crop coordinates are measured in real image pixels, so the output is exactly the size shown. Cropping copies the original pixels without resampling.

Save the result as PNG, JPG or WebP. Nothing is uploaded — the image is cropped locally on your device, and the tool works offline.

Frequently asked questions

How do I crop an image to a square?

Select the 1:1 preset. The crop box becomes the largest centered square; drag it to frame your subject and click Crop & download.

Can I enter exact crop dimensions?

Yes. Type X, Y, width and height in pixels in the number fields. Values are measured on the original image, so a 1080 × 1080 crop produces exactly a 1080 × 1080 file.

Does cropping reduce image quality?

No. Cropping copies the original pixels without resizing. PNG output is lossless; JPG and WebP apply compression at the quality you choose.

Does it work on phones and tablets?

Yes. The crop box uses pointer events, so you can drag it and its handles with a finger, a mouse or a stylus. It also works offline once the page has loaded.

Are my photos uploaded anywhere?

No. The image is loaded and cropped inside your browser using the canvas API, and it never leaves your device.
