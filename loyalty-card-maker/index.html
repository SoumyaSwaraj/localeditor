What it does

  • A stamp card with 5, 8 or 10 stamps and your reward, in four styles
  • Business-card size, front and back, with 3 mm bleed and crop marks
  • Stamp slots with numbers or a small picture: cup, star, heart, scissors, paw or pizza
  • A line for the holder's name, or names and card numbers filled from a list
  • Print sheets of fronts and backs with cut marks that line up when printed on both sides

How it works

  1. Fill in your business name, the number of stamps and the reward.
  2. Choose a style and a size, then tap Make it. The front and the back open in the editor as two pages.
  3. Add print sheets and print both sides at 100 percent, or download a PDF for a print shop.

Your files stay on this device

What you type, the photos you add and any list you bring stay on this device. The design, the QR codes and the exported files are made by your browser, and nothing is uploaded.

How do I print both sides so they line up?

Choose A4 or US Letter under Print sheets. You get a sheet of fronts and a sheet of backs. Print the fronts, put the paper back in and print the backs, flipping on the edge named in the note at the top of the back sheet. Print at 100 percent. The 3 mm bleed gives room for the small shift every printer has.

How many cards fit on a sheet?

Nine 85 × 55 mm cards on A4, or eight 3.5 × 2 in cards on US Letter.

Can I make named cards from a spreadsheet?

Yes. Tap Make one for each row of a list. Columns called name and no fill the holder's name and the card number on the back, and the button opens Create many on the back page for you. For most shops a blank name line is enough: leave the name empty and print one sheet.

Can I change the stamps or the wording?

Yes. The card opens in the editor as an ordinary design, so every slot, picture and line of text can be moved, recoloured or replaced. Edit details reopens the form.
