How to apply filters to a photo

  1. Drop a JPG, PNG or WebP photo into the box.
  2. Tap a preset or fine-tune the brightness, contrast, saturation, blur, grayscale, sepia, hue and invert sliders.
  3. Click Download to save the edited photo at its original resolution.

What is an online photo filter editor?

A photo filter editor changes the color and tone of an image by applying adjustments such as brightness, contrast, saturation, hue rotation, grayscale, sepia, invert and Gaussian blur. This tool uses the HTML5 Canvas 2D filter property, the same filter functions as CSS, so the preview and the exported file match exactly.

The live preview is rendered at screen size for speed, while the download is re-rendered at the photo's original pixel dimensions. Blur is scaled proportionally so it looks the same at full size. JPG photos are saved as JPG at 92% quality; PNG and WebP keep their format. In browsers without canvas filter support, a built-in pixel color matrix is used instead.

Your photos are processed only on your device. Nothing is uploaded, and the editor works offline.

Frequently asked questions

Will the downloaded photo keep its original resolution?

Yes. The preview is scaled to fit your screen, but the download is rendered at the photo's full original width and height.

Which filters and adjustments are available?

Brightness, contrast, saturation, blur, grayscale, sepia, hue rotation and invert, plus presets such as Vivid, Mono, Vintage, Warm, Cool, Fade, Noir and Dramatic.

Can I compare the edited photo with the original?

Yes. Press and hold the Compare button to temporarily see the original photo, then release to return to your edit.

Are my photos uploaded to a server?

No. All editing happens in your browser with the Canvas API. Your photos never leave your device, and the tool works offline.

Which image formats are supported?

Any image your browser can open, including JPG, PNG, WebP, GIF, AVIF and BMP. JPG input saves as JPG; other formats save as PNG or WebP.
