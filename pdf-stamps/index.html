More on the privacy and install page.

Is a stamp a signature?

No. A stamp is a picture of words with your name and date. For signing use the Sign tool. Whether either counts as binding depends on where you are and what you sign, and we cannot give legal advice.

Can I stamp every page?

Yes. Choose initials, the date or a signature, and apply it to every page or only the pages you select.

Does the comment summary include highlights?

It lists comments, notes and highlights, each with its page.
