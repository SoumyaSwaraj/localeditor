Turn a spreadsheet into a clear chart.

What it does

  • Import CSV, TSV or Excel, paste cells from a spreadsheet, or type the numbers in
  • Suggests fitting charts as live previews, each with a one-line reason
  • 23 chart types: bar and column, line and area, pie and donut, scatter, combo, waterfall, funnel, radar, gauge, heatmap, treemap and more
  • 9 themes, including one that takes its colours and fonts from your brand kit
  • Highlight one bar or line with a tap; bars are always drawn to scale

How it works

  1. Open the chart maker and bring your numbers.
  2. Pick a suggested chart, or choose the type yourself.
  3. Highlight what matters, pick a theme, then download or keep designing around it.

Your files stay on this device

Files are read by your browser on this device and nothing is uploaded. Cells are treated as text and numbers; formulas are never run.

Questions

Short, honest answers.

More on the privacy and install page.

Which files work?

CSV, TSV and Excel files, or cells pasted from any spreadsheet. The first row is read as the column names.

Can I edit the chart afterwards?

Yes. Edit chart changes the data, the type or the look in one step. Each part is also an ordinary layer you can move or restyle, though a later chart edit redraws it.

How much data can it take?

A chart in a design holds up to 5,000 cells. A larger import is added up or sampled, and the panel says which.

Can I make a poster with a headline and a source line?

Yes. The Chart poster maker adds a headline, a note and a source line around the chart in four sizes.

What happens when I resize a chart?

It stretches like any group. Open Edit chart to lay the labels out again at the new size.
