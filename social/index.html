Drafts stay private until you post

Drafts and unposted photos stay on your device until you choose to post them. Nothing is uploaded while you edit, there is no account to link, and no watermark is added to what you export.

Questions

Short, honest answers.

More on the privacy and install page.

Which sizes are included?

Instagram post, portrait and story, YouTube thumbnail, Facebook post and cover, LinkedIn post and banner, X post and header, Pinterest pin, plus print and screen sizes.

Can I change the size after I start?

Yes. Pick a new size and the design is resized from the original, so you can switch back without losing quality.

Can I post straight to Instagram?

Download the image and post it from your phone's gallery. The app never connects to your social accounts.
