What is in a recipe?

Just a list of edits

A .leworkflow file names a look, lists its steps (like “Warmth +12”) and suggests a strength. It holds no photo and no code. A typical recipe is under 2 KB.

Checked before it runs

LocalEditor accepts only edits from a fixed list, with every value inside its allowed range. Files with script, markup, unknown edits or anything over 2 MB are refused.

Private links

A share link carries the recipe after the #. Browsers never send that part to a server, so opening a recipe link uploads nothing, not even to us.
