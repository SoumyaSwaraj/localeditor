Fill forms from saved details

Type your details once, fill any form in seconds.

Save your name, address, contact details, work and tax details, bank details and any IDs you choose, and fill a form from them with a click. They are kept in this browser and nowhere else.

What it does

  • Name, address, contact, personal, work and tax, and bank details, grouped
  • IDs you choose to add, such as a passport, licence or staff number
  • Fill the fields of a form from your details, or click a detail and place it anywhere on a page
  • Your date of birth and today’s date for date fields, and a saved signature

How it works

  1. Open My details and add what you want to keep.
  2. Open a form in the PDF studio and choose Fill from my details.
  3. Check every field, then save the PDF.

Your files stay on this device

Your details are stored in this browser on this device and are never uploaded or logged. They sit in this browser’s storage as plain text, so anyone who can open this browser profile could read them. Use it on a device you control, and delete them when you no longer want them kept.

Questions

Short, honest answers.

More on the privacy and install page.

Does it guess the right field?

It matches field names and hints to your details, and shows each choice. It can get one wrong, so check every field before you save.

Can I keep details for more than one person?

Yes. You can save more than one set, for yourself, a partner or a company, and choose which to use.

How do I delete them?

In My details, or by clearing saved work in the storage manager.
