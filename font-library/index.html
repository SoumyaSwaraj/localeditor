What it does

  • 257 open-licence families, plus 20 originals made for LocalEditor

  • Suggested pairings for a title and its body text
  • Save fonts for offline use, one family or a whole collection, so a saved design always opens with its fonts

How it works

  1. Open the font list in the studio.
  2. Search or filter, and preview your own text in a family.
  3. Pick it, and save it for offline use if you want it without a connection.

Your files stay on this device

Fonts are served from this site and loaded only when a design uses them, so no font service learns what you write. Font files stay in this browser until you clear them in the storage manager.

Questions

Short, honest answers.

More on the privacy and install page.

Can I use these fonts commercially?

Every family is under an open licence (SIL Open Font Licence or Apache 2.0), which allows use in your own work. The licence texts are kept with each family, and the licences page lists them.

How much do they download?

Nothing at first. A family downloads when you use it, only the parts of it your text needs, and most are small. Saving a whole collection for offline use is bigger, and the storage manager shows the size.

Which languages are covered?

Latin, Cyrillic, Greek and Vietnamese, plus several Indian scripts, Arabic, Hebrew and Thai. A typeface that lacks a letter borrows it from a matching fallback.

Do the fonts work in PDFs?

Yes, in text you add in the PDF studio. Some scripts that need complex shaping, such as Devanagari and Bengali, can fall back to a standard font in a saved PDF.
