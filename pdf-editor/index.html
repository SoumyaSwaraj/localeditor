What it does

  • Draw, type or upload a signature and place it on any page
  • Fill form fields, or add text anywhere on the page
  • Highlight, draw, add shapes, images and comments
  • Merge files, and split, rotate, reorder or delete pages
  • Save a new PDF or export pages as images

How it works

  1. Open the PDF studio and drop in a PDF.
  2. Use Sign, Forms or Edit from the side rail.
  3. Save the PDF to your device.

Your files stay on this device

The PDF is opened and saved by your browser. It is not uploaded, so a signed contract or a filled form exists only where you saved it.

Questions

Short, honest answers.

More on the privacy and install page.

Is my signature stored?

Only if you choose to save it, and then only in this browser. You can remove it at any time.

Can it edit the existing text of a PDF?

You can add text and cover areas, but rewriting the original text of a PDF is limited. For a full rewrite, edit the source document and convert it again.

Is a drawn signature legally binding?

That depends on where you are and what you are signing. We cannot give legal advice; check with the person or organisation asking for the signature.
