Photobooth strip

Make a photobooth strip from your camera or your photos.

Use your camera with a countdown and retake any frame, or pick photos you already have. You get a strip, a 2 × 2 print or a wide strip, with a caption and a date line, and an optional sheet with two copies and a cut line.

Make a strip

FreeNo accountNothing uploaded

What it does

  • A strip of four or three frames (2 × 6 in), a 2 × 2 print on 4 × 6 in, or a wide strip (6 × 2 in), at 300 dpi
  • Camera with a countdown and a flash, and a retake for any single frame
  • Pick up to four photos instead, filled in order
  • One look for every frame: natural, black and white, warm, film, vintage or faded
  • Four styles, a caption, an optional date line, a mirror switch, and a print sheet with two copies and a cut line

How it works

  1. Open the maker and choose a size.
  2. Take the frames with the camera, or pick your photos.
  3. Add a caption, choose a look and a style, tap Make it, then download or print.

Your files stay on this device

Camera pictures stay on this device. The camera runs only while its panel is open and stops the moment you close it. Nothing is uploaded.

Questions

Short, honest answers.

More on the privacy and install page.

Will it ask to use my camera?

Yes, your browser asks, and you can refuse. Without a camera, or if you refuse, pick photos instead.

Are the frames mirrored?

They are saved the right way round unless you turn on Mirror.

What size does it print?

Print sizes are real: 2 × 6 inches for a strip, 4 × 6 for a 2 × 2 grid, 6 × 2 for the wide strip, all at 300 dpi. The sheet option puts two strips on a 4 × 6 page with a cut line.

Can I edit the result?

Yes. A maker produces ordinary layers. Tap any part to change it, move it or replace a photo, or use Edit details to reopen the form and remake the page.
