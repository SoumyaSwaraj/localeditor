What it does

  • Contact, cast, floating, studio and long shadows, each with its own softness, direction and strength
  • Floor, water and glass reflections
  • Refine edge, edge light and light wrap so a cut-out blends into its background
  • Match colour and ambient light to the new background
  • Everything stays an adjustable setting on the photo

How it works

  1. Cut the product out with the background remover, place it on its new background and open Expert mode.
  2. In the panel, open Shadows & reflections and add a contact shadow, then a cast shadow or a reflection. Open Compositing to refine the edge and match colour.
  3. Move the sliders until the product looks grounded, then download.

Questions

Short, honest answers.

More on the privacy and install page.

Does the photo need a transparent background?

Yes. Shadows and reflections follow the outline of the subject, so the product needs a cut-out or a transparent background. The background remover on this site makes one.

How do I place the shadow?

Each shadow has an anchor, the point where the product meets the ground, which you can drag on the picture. Put Refine edge first, then reflections, then shadows, so that a reflection does not reflect the shadow.

Are the results realistic?

A shadow is drawn from the outline of the product as if it stood on a flat floor. That suits packshots and simple scenes. It is not a 3D render, so a product with a complicated base, or a busy scene, needs adjusting by eye.

Can a big photo be slow?

Cast shadows and reflections are the most demanding effects. On large pictures they are drawn in tiles and can take a few seconds on a modest device.
