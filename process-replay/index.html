Process replay

Show how you made it, as a short video.

Replay the edits you made, in order, and save them as a short video for a reel or a story. If no edits were recorded, the layers build in one by one instead.

What it does

  • Plays back the edits made in this session, in order
  • Or builds the design layer by layer, back to front
  • Choose a length from 6 to 20 seconds, a pace, and how long to hold the last frame
  • MP4 or WebM, depending on what your browser can record, at 30 frames a second
  • An optional “Made with LocalEditor” end tag, off by default

How it works

  1. Make or open a design and edit it.
  2. Open Download, choose Replay, and set a length and pace.
  3. Make the replay, watch it, then download or share it.

Your files stay on this device

The video is recorded on this device. The edit history behind it is kept in memory for this session only.

Questions

Short, honest answers.

More on the privacy and install page.

Which video format do I get?

MP4 or WebM, depending on your browser. The panel shows the format and the file size before you save. If the browser cannot record video, you can download the frames as a zip of JPEGs instead.

Is my edit history saved?

No. Recorded edits last for this session. After you reload, the replay builds the design layer by layer instead.

Does it include my photos?

Yes, as they appear in the design, drawn on your device.
