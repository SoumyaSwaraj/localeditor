Questions

Before you start

Short answers about privacy, offline use, and recipes.

Does a photo leave this device?

No. Editing uses the browser on your computer or phone. There is no account and no upload.

Will it work offline?

After the first visit, the app can be installed and the tools that have already loaded keep working without a connection.

What is a recipe?

A recipe is a small .leworkflow file: a list of edits such as warmth, contrast, and sharpen. You can preview it, apply it, change the steps, and send the file to someone else. It cannot contain scripts.

Can I remove a background?

You can remove a flat color or paint the background away. Automatic AI removal is not included, and a recipe that asks for it will tell you before it continues.
