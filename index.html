Frequently asked questions

Are these tools really free?

Yes. All 30 tools are completely free with no sign-up, no watermarks on your output, no daily limits and no ads in the workflow.

Are my files uploaded to a server?

No. Every tool runs entirely inside your browser using JavaScript, Canvas, Web Crypto, pdf-lib and pdf.js. Your images, PDFs and text never leave your device.

Can I use the tools offline?

Yes. Instant Tools is a Progressive Web App. After your first visit, every tool is cached on your device and keeps working without an internet connection.

How do I install Instant Tools on my phone?

On Android (Chrome, Edge, Samsung Internet) tap "Install app" or use the browser menu → "Install app". On iPhone or iPad open the site in Safari, tap Share → "Add to Home Screen". It then opens full-screen like a native app.

Which browsers are supported?

Any modern browser: Chrome, Edge, Safari, Firefox, Samsung Internet and Opera on desktop, Android and iOS.

Is there a file size limit?

There is no artificial limit. Because processing happens on your device, the practical limit depends on your device memory — typically hundreds of MB on desktop and tens of MB on phones.
