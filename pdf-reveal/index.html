What does this PDF reveal?

See what a PDF carries before you send it.

Lists what travels with a PDF besides the visible pages, in plain language: who made it and when, comments, attached files, form answers, hidden text, earlier versions and more. Then you can remove it in one step.

What it does

  • Who made it, with what software, and when
  • Comments, attached files, form answers and links to outside addresses
  • Text you cannot see: invisible, white on white, off the page, or under a picture or shape
  • Earlier versions and unused leftovers still inside the file
  • Page previews, and camera or location details in pictures

How it works

  1. Open the PDF, choose Protect, then What does this PDF reveal?
  2. Read the report, grouped by kind of detail.
  3. Remove what you do not want to send, or all of it, and download the cleaned copy.

Questions

Short, honest answers.

More on the privacy and install page.

Does removing this change the pages?

No. It removes data around the pages, not the content you see. The check reads the file as you opened it, not later edits in the studio.

Is every hidden item found?

The common ones are. An unusual file may hold something the check does not recognise, so read important files yourself too.

What about a password-protected PDF?

The details can be read in some cases, but they can only be removed once the password has been taken off the file.
