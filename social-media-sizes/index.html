Sizes for every platform

The right size for every platform.

Choose a size before you start or change it later. The design is resized from the original each time, so switching back and forth does not blur it.

What it does

  • Instagram post, portrait and story or reel
  • YouTube thumbnail, Facebook post and cover, LinkedIn post and banner
  • X post and header, Pinterest pin
  • A4, US Letter, business card, slides and phone wallpaper
  • Your own width and height in pixels

How it works

  1. Open the sizes list and pick where the design is going.
  2. Design as usual; the page shows the exact pixel size.
  3. Switch sizes at any time and download.

Your files stay on this device

Resizing happens in your browser. Nothing is uploaded, and the size list is part of the app, so it works offline.

Will changing the size stretch my design?

No. The design is resized from the original layout, and you can reset it if you do not like the result.

Can I resize a single photo instead?

Yes. The image resizer tool changes the pixel size of a photo in one step.
