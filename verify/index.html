What a match proves, and what it does not

A match proves the file you dropped is byte for byte the same file the receipt was made from: nothing in it has been added, removed or changed since then.

It does not prove who made the file, when it was first created, or that what it says is true. Anyone can make a receipt for any file. A different fingerprint only tells you the files are not identical; re-saving or re-printing a document to PDF changes the fingerprint even when the text looks the same.

How it works

  1. Someone makes a fingerprint receipt in the PDF studio (Office kit → Fingerprint receipt). It shows a SHA-256 fingerprint and a QR code.
  2. You scan the QR code or open its link. The fingerprint is in the part of the link after the #, which browsers keep on your device.
  3. Drop your copy of the file here. This page works out its fingerprint and compares the two.
