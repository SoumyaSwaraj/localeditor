How to rotate PDF pages

  1. Drop a PDF into the box, or tap to choose one.
  2. Rotate single pages with their arrow buttons, or use Rotate all for every page.
  3. Press Save rotated PDF to download the result.

What is a PDF rotator?

A PDF rotator changes the orientation of pages in a PDF. This tool sets each page’s /Rotate value in 90-degree steps and adds it to any rotation the page already has, so sideways scans and upside-down pages are fixed permanently.

Rotation is lossless: page content is not re-rendered, so text stays selectable and images keep full quality. Thumbnails are drawn locally with pdf.js and the file is saved with pdf-lib. Your PDF never leaves your device and the tool works offline.

Frequently asked questions

How do I rotate just one page of a PDF?

Open your PDF, then use the rotate left or rotate right buttons under that page. Other pages stay unchanged. Press Save rotated PDF when you are done.

Can I rotate all pages at once?

Yes. Use Rotate all left, Rotate all right or Rotate all 180° to turn every page in one click, then save.

Is the rotation permanent?

Yes. The saved PDF stores the new orientation, so it opens rotated in every PDF reader, browser and printer.

Does rotating a PDF reduce quality?

No. Only the page rotation setting changes. Text, fonts and images are kept exactly as they were.

Is my file uploaded?

No. The PDF is processed entirely in your browser and never leaves your device. The tool also works offline.
