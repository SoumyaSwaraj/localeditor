What is Case Converter?

A case converter changes the capitalization or word separators of text without retyping it. This tool offers 11 formats: UPPERCASE, lowercase, Title Case, Sentence case, camelCase, PascalCase, snake_case, kebab-case, CONSTANT_CASE, aLtErNaTiNg case and iNVERSE case.

Title Case follows common style-guide rules: small words such as “a”, “and”, “of”, “the” and “to” stay lowercase unless they are the first or last word. Programming cases (camelCase, snake_case and so on) split text on spaces, punctuation and existing case changes, so “userID value” becomes user_id_value. Conversion is Unicode-aware and handles accented letters.

Everything runs in your browser with JavaScript; your text is never sent anywhere and the tool works offline.

Frequently asked questions

What is the difference between Title Case and Sentence case?

Title Case capitalizes the first letter of each major word, as in a headline. Sentence case capitalizes only the first letter of each sentence, like normal prose.

Which words stay lowercase in Title Case?

Articles, short conjunctions and short prepositions such as a, an, the, and, but, or, for, nor, of, on, in, at, to, by, as and vs stay lowercase unless they are the first or last word.

What is camelCase vs PascalCase?

camelCase joins words with no spaces and capitalizes every word except the first, like myVariableName. PascalCase also capitalizes the first word, like MyVariableName.

What are snake_case, kebab-case and CONSTANT_CASE?

snake_case joins lowercase words with underscores, kebab-case joins them with hyphens, and CONSTANT_CASE joins uppercase words with underscores. They are common for variables, URLs and constants.

Is my text uploaded?

No. All conversion happens locally in your browser with JavaScript, so your text never leaves your device and the tool works offline.
