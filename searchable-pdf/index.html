Searchable scans

Make a scanned PDF searchable, on this device.

Reads the text in scanned pages with a text reader that runs on this device, so you can search, copy and redact it. The page keeps its picture, and an invisible text layer is added.

What it does

  • Reads the text in scanned pages on this device, in English and in any other language this copy of the app offers
  • Adds an invisible text layer, so you can search and copy while the page looks the same
  • Quality choices: Faster, Standard and Sharper (computers only), and by default only pages with no text
  • Reports pages read, words found, uncertain words and pages that were hard to read
  • Copy the text or save it as a .txt file

How it works

  1. Open the scanned PDF in the PDF studio. If pages have no text, a bar offers Make searchable.
  2. Choose Make searchable, pick the language and quality, and wait. The first time, the text reader is fetched from this site, with a progress card.
  3. Search, copy or save the text, or save the PDF. Cancel stops it at once and leaves the document untouched.

Your files stay on this device

The scan is read by an engine that runs in your browser on this device, and is never uploaded. The text reader and its English data download from this site once.

Questions

Short, honest answers.

More on the privacy and install page.

How accurate is it?

It depends on the scan. Clear, straight, printed text reads best. Handwriting, small print, skewed or blurry pages and unusual fonts read worse. Uncertain words are counted, and hard pages are flagged with a suggestion to try a sharper quality. Check important text yourself.

Which languages?

English is included. The panel lists any other languages this copy of the app has. One language is read per run.

Does it change my pages?

The page picture stays as it is. The open document is replaced by its searchable copy as one undo step, so you can undo it.

Can it help find sensitive data in scans?

Yes. Find sensitive data can read the scanned pages first and marks those matches for you to check.
