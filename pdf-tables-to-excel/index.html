PDF tables to a spreadsheet

Pull tables out of a PDF into a spreadsheet.

Open a PDF, find the tables on its pages and save them as an Excel file or a CSV. It works from where the text sits on the page, and it is best effort: check the result.

What it does

  • Finds tables from the position of the text on a page
  • Save as Excel (.xlsx) or CSV
  • Choose a page or all pages, or draw a box around one table
  • Joins a table that carries on across pages

How it works

  1. Open Business docs and choose Extract tables to a spreadsheet.
  2. Choose the PDF, then the pages, or a box around the table.
  3. Check the preview and save as Excel or CSV.

Your files stay on this device

The files are read and written by your browser on this device. Nothing is uploaded.

Questions

Short, honest answers.

More on the privacy and install page.

How accurate is it?

It depends on the PDF. Tables with clear columns and rules work well. Merged cells, tables without clear columns and pictures of tables can come out wrong, so always check the numbers.

Does it work on scans?

Not directly. A scan is a picture with no text positions. Make it searchable first, then extract.

Which spreadsheet apps open the result?

Any that opens .xlsx or CSV files.
