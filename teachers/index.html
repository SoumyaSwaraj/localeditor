Student details stay on this device

Student names, marks, photos and reports are opened and edited by your browser on this device. They are not uploaded to us or to anyone else, and there is no account to create. Your school's own rules on where student data may be stored still apply, so check them before you save files on a shared or personal device.

Questions

Short, honest answers.

More on the privacy and install page.

Do students or I need an account?

No. Nobody signs in, and nothing is sent to a server, so there is no student account to set up or remove.

Can I use it on a school computer?

Yes, in any current browser. If the computer is shared, remember that saved designs stay in that browser until you clear them in the storage manager.

Is this approved for use with student data?

We do not claim any certification. The tools are designed so that files stay on the device, but whether that meets your school's policy is for your school to decide.
