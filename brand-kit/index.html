Brand kit

Your colours, fonts and logo, one tap away.

Keep your brand's colours, fonts and logos in one place. They appear first whenever you pick a colour or a font, so every design matches.

Open the Brand kit

FreeNo accountNothing uploaded

What it does

  • Brand colours at the top of every colour picker
  • Brand fonts at the top of the font list
  • Logos ready to drop into any design
  • Pull colours from a photo or a logo
  • Replace colours or fonts across a whole design in one step

How it works

  1. Open the Brand kit in the studio.
  2. Add your colours, fonts and logo.
  3. Pick them from the top of any colour or font list.

Your files stay on this device

Your brand kit is stored in this browser only. It is not synced to an account, because there is no account.

Can I use my brand kit on another device?

Not automatically. Save a project that uses your brand, and open it on the other device.

How do I clear it?

Edit the brand kit in the studio, or clear saved work in the storage manager.
