Remove a background on your own device.

Remove the background from a photo with a model that runs in your browser. It works best on one clear subject, such as a person, a pet or a product, and you can fix the edges with a brush.

What it does

  • Three quality options: Quick (about 5 MB), More detail and People and portraits (about 44 MB each)
  • Says whether this device can run each option before you download it
  • Brush to erase or restore edges, or to cut a subject out by hand
  • Put the subject on a transparent, colour or blurred backdrop, with an optional outline
  • Your original photo is kept: show it again at any time

How it works

  1. Open a photo in the studio, then choose Remove background.
  2. The first time, a one-off model download is explained before it starts. Then the cut-out runs on this device.
  3. Touch up the edges with the brush, choose a backdrop and download.

Your files stay on this device

The model is downloaded to this browser once and runs on this device. Your photo is never sent to a server. The model is listed in the storage manager, where you can delete it.

Questions

Short, honest answers.

More on the privacy and install page.

Why is it labelled Beta?

Results depend on the photo. It works best on one clear subject that stands out from its background. Crowded scenes, fine hair and glass can need touching up with the brush.

How big is the download?

Quick is about 5 MB. More detail and People and portraits are about 44 MB each. The panel tells you before anything downloads, and after that the model works offline.

What if it cannot find a subject?

It says so and changes nothing. You can try the More detail option, or cut the subject out by hand with the brush.

Does it change my original photo?

No. The cut-out is a mask on top of the photo, so you can switch back to the original at any time.
