Recipes and batch editing

Save a look as a recipe, apply it to every photo.

A recipe is a short list of edits such as warmth, contrast and vignette. Save one from your own edit or start from a built-in recipe, then apply it to a batch and export them together.

What it does

  • Built-in recipes to start from, and your own saved looks
  • Edit, reorder and switch off steps, with a strength slider
  • Apply to a batch of photos and export them together
  • Share a recipe as a file or a link; the link keeps the recipe in the part of the address that is never sent to a server
  • Recipes are plain data: they can never run code

How it works

  1. Pick a recipe, or edit a photo and save the look as your own.
  2. Add the photos you want to change and check the preview.
  3. Apply and export them all, or share the recipe with someone else.

Your files stay on this device

Batch edits run on this device, one photo after another. A shared recipe contains only the list of edits, never your photos.

More on the privacy and install page.

What is in a recipe file?

A short .leworkflow file listing operations and values, such as contrast 16 and warmth 14. It is readable text and cannot contain scripts.

How many photos can I batch at once?

As many as your device handles comfortably. Large batches are faster on a computer.
