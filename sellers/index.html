Questions

Short, honest answers.

More on the privacy and install page.

Do I need an account or a subscription?

No. Everything is free, with no sign-up and no watermarks.

Does it connect to my shop?

No. It makes images and documents on your device. You upload them to your shop yourself.

Can it make a white-background product photo?

It cuts the subject out and puts it on a white or coloured background. Results are best with one clear subject, and the brush lets you fix the edges.
