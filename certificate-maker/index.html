Certificate maker

Make a certificate, one or a whole class.

Fill in a short form and get a certificate with signature lines and a seal, ready to print. The recipient's name, the reason and the date are fields, so one more step fills a certificate for every row of a class or staff list.

What it does

  • Achievement, completion, participation, appreciation and attendance certificates in six styles
  • A4 and US Letter, wide or tall, at print resolution, plus a screen size for sharing
  • Up to two signature lines with names and roles, an optional logo, and a seal or ribbon
  • The name shrinks to fit its line. Name, reason and date are fields you can fill from a list
  • Exports: PNG or JPG, a PDF at the real paper size, or one certificate per row as a zip, one PDF or print sheets

How it works

  1. Fill in the recipient, the reason, the date and who signs.
  2. Choose a style and a paper size, then tap Make it. The certificate opens in the editor, where every part can be changed.
  3. Download a PDF or an image, or tap Make one for each row of a list to fill it from a class list.

Your files stay on this device

What you type, the photos you add and any list you bring stay on this device. The design, the QR codes and the exported files are made by your browser, and nothing is uploaded.

Questions

Short, honest answers.

More on the privacy and install page.

Can I make one for each person from a spreadsheet?

Yes. In the form, tap Make one for each row of a list. It makes the certificate and opens Create many, where you paste cells from a spreadsheet or choose a CSV, TSV or Excel file. A column called name fills the recipient, and columns called reason and awarded fill the reason and the date. You get a zip of images, one PDF, print sheets or pages in the design.

Which sizes are there?

A4 and US Letter, each wide or tall, at 300 dots per inch (A4 wide is 3508 × 2480 pixels), and a 1600 × 1131 screen size for messages and email.

What happens with a very long name?

The name shrinks to stay on its line. On tall pages a long name may take two lines instead.

Will it print at the right size?

Yes, if you choose actual size or 100 percent in the print dialog. The PDF page is the paper size. Most home printers leave a white edge of a few millimetres, so the borders sit well inside the page.

Can I change the result?

Yes. It opens in the editor as an ordinary design. Tap any part to change it, or use Edit details to reopen the form and remake it in one undo step. If a design is already open, the new pages are added after the page you are on.
