Quick makers

Fill in a form, get an editable design.

Pick a maker, fill in a few fields, add photos if it asks for them, and get a finished design made of ordinary layers that you can keep editing in the studio. There are 36, from quote cards and movie posters to certificates, business cards, calendars and photo books.

What it does

  • 36 makers in six groups: Social, Business, Life, Photo, Print and Events
  • Four to eight styles each, in square, portrait and story sizes, or real paper sizes for printables
  • A live preview while you fill in the form
  • The result is a normal design: tap any part to edit it, and Edit details reopens the form to remake the page
  • Print makers come at real paper sizes with cut and fold guides where they are needed

How it works

  1. Pick a maker from the gallery.
  2. Fill in the fields, add a photo if you like, and choose a style and size.
  3. Tap Make it, then edit any part or download.

Your files stay on this device

What you type and the photos you add stay on this device. The preview and the finished design are drawn in your browser.

Questions

Short, honest answers.

More on the privacy and install page.

Can I edit the result?

Yes. A maker produces ordinary layers. Tap any part to change it, or use Edit details to reopen the form.

Do makers use my brand kit?

No. Some makers have an accent colour that you can set yourself.

Can I link straight to a maker?

Yes. Add ?make= and the maker name to the studio address, for example /studio/?make=quote.

Can an AI agent use them?

Yes. Makers are part of the agent operations and the command line, and a maker’s form values are stored with the design.
