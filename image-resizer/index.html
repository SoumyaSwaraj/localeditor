How to resize an image

  1. Drop an image onto the box or tap to choose one.
  2. Enter a width and height in pixels, a percentage, or pick a preset such as 1080 × 1080 or 1920 × 1080.
  3. Choose the output format and click Download resized image.

What is an Image Resizer?

An image resizer changes the pixel dimensions of a picture. This tool scales your image on an HTML canvas with high-quality smoothing and multi-step downsampling, so large reductions stay sharp instead of jagged.

Resize by exact pixels (up to 16,384 px per side) or by percentage (1–400%). With the aspect ratio locked, changing the width updates the height automatically so nothing is stretched. When a preset has a different shape than your photo, choose Cover (crop to fill), Contain (fit with padding) or Stretch.

Export as JPG, PNG or WebP. Everything happens on your device — no upload, no sign-up, and it works offline.

Frequently asked questions

How do I resize an image without stretching it?

Keep Lock aspect ratio switched on. When you type a new width, the height is calculated from the original proportions automatically, so the picture is never distorted.

How do I resize a photo to 1080×1080 for Instagram?

Choose the 1080 × 1080 preset. If your photo is not square, pick Cover to crop it to fill the square or Contain to fit the whole photo with padding, then download.

Does resizing reduce file size?

Yes. Making an image smaller in pixels reduces its file size, often dramatically: halving the width and height leaves a quarter of the pixels. For even smaller files, export as JPG or WebP with a lower quality.

Can I make an image bigger?

Yes, up to 400% or 16,384 pixels per side. Upscaling cannot add detail that was not in the original, so very large enlargements will look soft.

Are my images uploaded?

No. Resizing happens entirely in your browser using the canvas API. Your image never leaves your device and the tool keeps working offline.
