Flyer maker

A flyer in a minute, for the wall or the feed.

A headline, a line under it, up to three points, when and where, a button-style call to action and your photo, laid out in six styles. Add a link and the flyer carries a QR code for it.

What it does

  • Six styles: clean, bold, photo poster, editorial, minimal and bright
  • A5, A4 and US Letter at print resolution, and a 1080 × 1350 size for social posts
  • Headline, a line under it, up to three points, when, where, a call to action and a contact line
  • A photo through the studio's normal import, and a QR code when you paste a link
  • Exports: PNG or JPG for sharing, or a PDF at the real paper size

How it works

  1. Type the headline, the points, and when and where.
  2. Add a photo and a link if you want a QR code, choose a style and a size, then tap Make it.
  3. The flyer opens in the editor. Change anything, then download a PDF to print or an image to post.

Your files stay on this device

What you type, the photos you add and any list you bring stay on this device. The design, the QR codes and the exported files are made by your browser, and nothing is uploaded.

Questions

Short, honest answers.

More on the privacy and install page.

Which sizes are there?

A5, A4 and US Letter at 300 dots per inch, and 1080 × 1350 pixels for social posts. There is no bleed, so on a home printer expect a white edge of a few millimetres around photos and colour that run to the edge.

What does the QR code open?

The link you paste, exactly as typed, with https:// added if you leave it off. Nothing is looked up. Very long links make dense codes that scan badly from paper, and the form says so.

What if my text is too long?

Everything shrinks together to fit. Past a point the form says which field to shorten, and lines that still do not fit are left off the page so nothing overlaps.

Can I make these from a spreadsheet?

A flyer is one design, so this maker has no list button. For many versions with different names, numbers or codes, use the certificate, ticket or voucher makers, or add fields to the finished flyer with the Fields panel and use Create many.

Can I change the result?

Yes. It opens in the editor as an ordinary design. Tap any part to change it, or use Edit details to reopen the form and remake it in one undo step. If a design is already open, the new pages are added after the page you are on.
