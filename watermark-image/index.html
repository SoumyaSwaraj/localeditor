How to add a watermark to an image

  1. Drop one or more JPG, PNG or WebP images into the box.
  2. Choose a text or logo watermark and set position, opacity, size, color and rotation.
  3. Click Download to save each watermarked image, or Download all for the whole batch.

What is Add Watermark to Image?

An image watermark is a semi-transparent text or logo drawn on top of a photo to mark ownership and discourage reuse. This tool draws the watermark with the HTML5 Canvas API at the image's full original resolution, so no quality is lost beyond the chosen export format.

You can place the mark in any of 9 grid positions or repeat it as a diagonal tile across the whole image. Watermark size scales with each image's width, so a batch of mixed sizes looks consistent. Supported inputs are JPG, PNG, WebP, GIF (first frame) and BMP; output keeps the original format where possible (JPG stays JPG at 92% quality, others export as PNG).

All processing happens locally on your device. Images are never uploaded to a server, and the tool keeps working offline once the page has loaded.

Frequently asked questions

Is this watermark tool free?

Yes. It is completely free with no sign-up, no limits on the number of images and no watermark of our own added to your output.

Are my photos uploaded anywhere?

No. Watermarks are drawn in your browser with the Canvas API. Your images never leave your device, and the tool works offline.

Can I use my logo as a watermark?

Yes. Switch to Logo mode and choose a PNG with transparency for the best result. You can scale, rotate, fade and tile the logo just like text.

Can I watermark many images at once?

Yes. Drop multiple images and the same watermark settings are applied to each one. Use Download all to save the whole batch.

Does adding a watermark reduce image quality?

The image is processed at its original resolution. PNG output is lossless; JPG output is re-encoded at 92% quality, which is visually indistinguishable for most photos.
