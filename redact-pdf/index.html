PDF redaction

Redact a PDF for real, then check it.

Draw boxes over what must go, or find sensitive data first. Redact and verify makes a new copy with the covered content removed, then opens that copy again and checks that nothing from under the boxes is left. Your original is not changed.

What it does

  • Draw boxes on any page, or add the matches from Find sensitive data
  • By default, pages with boxes are rebuilt as a picture of the page, so nothing under a box can be copied or recovered; their text can no longer be selected
  • Optional: keep other text selectable where a box covers only plain text
  • A verify report: the saved file is opened again and searched, and each box is checked to be solid black
  • Choose picture quality (Standard, High or Best) and an optional label such as REDACTED

How it works

  1. Open the PDF, choose Protect, then Hide information for good.
  2. Draw boxes over what to hide, or use Find sensitive data to mark it.
  3. Choose Redact and verify, read the report, and download the new copy.

Your files stay on this device

The file is redacted and checked on this device and never uploaded. Your original stays as it is; the redacted copy is a new file.

Questions

Short, honest answers.

More on the privacy and install page.

Why not just draw a black box?

A box drawn on top can be moved or deleted, and the text under it can still be copied. Redaction removes that content from the file.

What does “verified” check?

The saved copy is opened again. It checks that the page count is unchanged, that no text is left under a box, that the hidden words are not stored anywhere in the file, that every box is solid black and that no earlier version is inside. If a check fails it says so and tells you not to send the file. It does not replace reading the result yourself.

Why can I not select text on a redacted page?

Pages with boxes become pictures by default, so nothing under a box can be recovered. Pages without boxes stay as they were. The option to keep other text selectable keeps text only where a box covers plain text.

Can a redaction be undone after saving?

No. Keep your original if you might need it. A PDF locked with a password has to be unlocked first.
