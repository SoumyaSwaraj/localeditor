Printables

Calendars, charts, labels and books, ready to print.

Six makers for things you print and use: a month or week calendar, chore and reward charts, label and gift-tag sheets, sticker sheets, photo books and a one-sheet mini zine. Pages come at real paper sizes.

What it does

  • Calendar or weekly planner for any month, with your own events, in four paper layouts
  • Chore and reward charts, one page per child, with tick boxes or stars to fill
  • Label sheets and gift tags with a punch hole, and sticker sheets with cut lines
  • Photo books with a cover, varied photo pages, captions, page numbers and a back cover
  • A mini zine: eight little pages on one sheet that you fold and cut once. Most have an ink-saver style

How it works

  1. Pick the maker and fill in the form: the month, the names, the words or your photos.
  2. Choose a style and a paper size. A4 and US Letter are offered for most.
  3. Tap Make it, check the preview, then print at actual size or save as a PDF.

Your files stay on this device

What you type and the photos you add stay on this device. The preview and the finished design are drawn by your browser, and nothing is uploaded.

Questions

Short, honest answers.

More on the privacy and install page.

Which paper sizes are there?

A4 and US Letter for calendars, charts, labels and stickers, A4 or Letter landscape for the zine, and A5, a 20 cm square or A4 landscape for photo books.

Will it print at the right size?

Pages are made at print resolution on the chosen paper. In the print dialog choose actual size or 100 percent, not fit to page, so labels and cut lines keep their dimensions.

How do I fold the zine?

Print it on one sheet and fold it in half. Make the one cut along the middle of the fold, as the guides on the page show, then fold the pages into a little book. The guides can be switched off.

Can I edit the result?

Yes. A maker produces ordinary layers. Tap any part to change it, move it or replace a photo, or use Edit details to reopen the form and remake the page.
