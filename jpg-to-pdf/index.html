How to convert JPG to PDF

  1. Drop your JPG, PNG or WebP images into the box, or tap to choose them.
  2. Reorder the pages and choose page size, orientation and margin.
  3. Press Convert to PDF and download your document.

What is a JPG to PDF converter?

A JPG to PDF converter places each image on its own PDF page. This tool accepts JPG, PNG and WebP files, lets you pick A4 (210 × 297 mm), US Letter (8.5 × 11 in) or a page sized exactly to each image, and scales images to fit without cropping or stretching.

JPG photos are embedded in their original compression, so there is no quality loss. PNG keeps transparency, and WebP is converted to PNG in the browser because the PDF format does not support WebP. Everything runs locally with pdf-lib — no upload, and it works offline.

Frequently asked questions

How do I convert JPG to PDF?

Add your images, arrange them in order, choose page size, orientation and margin, then press Convert to PDF. The PDF downloads straight to your device.

Can I combine several images into one PDF?

Yes. Every image becomes one page of the same PDF, in the order shown. Drag thumbnails or use the arrow buttons to reorder them.

Does converting reduce image quality?

No. JPG images are embedded with their original data, and PNG images are stored losslessly. Only WebP is re-encoded, losslessly, as PNG.

Are my photos uploaded?

No. Conversion happens entirely in your browser. Your images never leave your device, and the tool works offline after it has loaded once.

What page sizes are supported?

A4, US Letter and Fit to image, where each page matches the image dimensions. Orientation can be automatic, portrait or landscape.
