What is Word & Character Counter?

A word counter is a tool that counts the words, characters, sentences and paragraphs in a piece of text. This one updates on every keystroke and runs entirely in your browser, so your text is never uploaded to a server.

Words are counted as runs of letters or numbers (apostrophes and hyphens inside a word keep it as one word), and it handles accented and non-Latin scripts. Reading time assumes 238 words per minute and speaking time assumes 140 words per minute — common averages for adult silent reading and presentations. Keyword density lists the top 10 words, excluding common English stopwords such as “the”, “and” and “of”.

Your draft is autosaved in your browser's local storage on this device only, so it survives a page reload. The tool works offline once loaded and has no length limit beyond your device's memory.

Frequently asked questions

How are words counted?

A word is any run of letters or digits, including apostrophes and hyphens inside it, so “don't” and “well-known” each count as one word. Punctuation and extra spaces are ignored.

How is reading time calculated?

Reading time divides the word count by 238 words per minute, the average adult silent reading speed. Speaking time uses 140 words per minute, a typical presentation pace.

Does the character count include spaces?

Both are shown. “Characters” counts every character including spaces and line breaks, and “without spaces” excludes all whitespace.

What is keyword density?

Keyword density is how often a word appears as a percentage of all words. The table shows your top 10 words, excluding common stopwords like “the”, “and” and “is”.

Is my text saved or uploaded?

Your text is never uploaded. A draft is saved only in your browser's local storage on this device so it survives a reload; click Clear to remove it.

Does the word counter work offline?

Yes. After the first visit the page is cached, so you can count words with no internet connection, including from the installed app.
