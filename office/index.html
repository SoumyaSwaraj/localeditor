Confidential files never leave this device

Contracts, payslips and personal records are processed by your browser on this computer. They are not uploaded, logged or kept by us. That removes one place a file can leak, but it does not by itself make your process compliant with any law or policy; follow your organisation's rules on handling personal data.

More on the privacy and install page.

Can IT check that nothing is uploaded?

Yes. Open the browser's developer tools, watch the network tab and edit a file: no request carries it. You can also disconnect from the network after the page loads and keep working.

Does it work without installing software?

Yes. It runs in Chrome, Edge, Safari or Firefox. It can be installed as an app for offline use, but that is optional.

Is this certified for regulated data?

No. We do not claim any certification or legal compliance. The design keeps files on the device; whether that is enough for your work is for your organisation to decide.
