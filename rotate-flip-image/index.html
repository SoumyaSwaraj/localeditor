How to rotate and flip an image

  1. Drop an image onto the box or tap to choose one.
  2. Use the rotate and flip buttons, or drag the angle slider to straighten the photo; the preview updates live.
  3. Choose the output format and click Download.

What is a Rotate & Flip Image tool?

A rotate and flip tool changes an image’s orientation. Rotating by 90°, 180° or 270° keeps every pixel: the canvas simply swaps width and height. Flipping mirrors the image horizontally (left to right) or vertically (top to bottom), which is useful for selfies, scans and design assets.

Custom angles from −180° to 180° in 0.5° steps help straighten tilted horizons. For angles that are not multiples of 90°, the canvas grows to fit the whole rotated image and the corners are left transparent (PNG and WebP) or filled with the background color you choose.

Export as PNG, JPG or WebP. All processing runs in your browser — nothing is uploaded, and the tool works offline.

Frequently asked questions

How do I rotate an image 90 degrees?

Click Rotate right to turn it 90° clockwise or Rotate left for 90° counter-clockwise. Click again for 180° or 270°, then download.

How do I mirror or flip a photo?

Click Flip horizontal to mirror it left to right, as is often needed for selfies, or Flip vertical to mirror it top to bottom.

How do I straighten a crooked photo?

Drag the custom angle slider or type an angle such as -2.5. The preview updates live. Then use the Image Cropper to trim the corners if you do not want them.

What happens to the corners when I rotate by a custom angle?

The canvas expands to fit the whole rotated image. Corners stay transparent in PNG and WebP; in JPG they are filled with the background color you pick.

Is rotating an image lossless?

Rotations of 90°, 180° and 270° and flips keep every pixel, and saving as PNG is fully lossless. JPG and WebP output apply compression at the chosen quality.
