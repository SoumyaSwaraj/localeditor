Ticket maker

Event tickets, numbered and ready to tear.

A ticket with the event, date, time, venue, seat and price, and a stub with the number again as a QR code. The number and the seat are fields, so a list makes the whole run.

What it does

  • A ticket with a tear-off stub and a dashed perforation line, in four styles
  • Event, date, time, venue, seat or zone, price and a ticket number
  • The number as a QR code on the stub
  • Three sizes: 140 × 55 mm, 5.5 × 2 in and 180 × 70 mm, with a print sheet and cut marks
  • A numbered run from a list: one ticket per row, each with its own number, code and seat

How it works

  1. Fill in the event, the date and time, the venue and the price.
  2. Choose a style and a size, then tap Make it. The ticket opens in the editor.
  3. Tap Make one for each row of a list for a numbered run, then save print sheets or one PDF.

Your files stay on this device

What you type, the photos you add and any list you bring stay on this device. The design, the QR codes and the exported files are made by your browser, and nothing is uploaded.

Questions

Short, honest answers.

More on the privacy and install page.

Can I make a numbered run from a spreadsheet?

Yes. Tap Make one for each row of a list. Create many takes a list with a column called no for the ticket number and, if you like, one called seat. Type the first numbers in a spreadsheet, fill them down, then paste the cells or choose the CSV or Excel file. The QR code on each ticket holds that ticket's number. Save print sheets with cut marks, one PDF or a zip of images.

Is the perforation real?

No. The dashed line shows where the stub comes off. Fold and tear along it, cut it, or ask a print shop to perforate there.

Does the QR code check people in?

The code holds only the ticket number as text. Any QR reader shows it, which is enough to tick numbers off a list. It is not tied to a ticketing system and does not stop a copied ticket.

How many tickets fit on a sheet?

Four 140 × 55 mm tickets on A4, at full size with a margin for the printer.

Can I change the result?

Yes. It opens in the editor as an ordinary design. Tap any part to change it, or use Edit details to reopen the form and remake it in one undo step. If a design is already open, the new pages are added after the page you are on.
