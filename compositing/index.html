What it does

  • 27 blend modes, with fill opacity and blend-if ranges
  • Masks: brush, linear and radial gradients, brightness range, colour range, subject and depth
  • Match colour, ambient light and light wrap to join a subject to a background
  • Adjustment layers that affect everything beneath them

How it works

  1. Add your photos as layers in Expert mode and select the top one.
  2. Set its blend mode in the Layers panel, or add Match colour from Compositing in the Develop panel.
  3. Paint or draw a mask where the layer should show, then download.

Questions

Short, honest answers.

More on the privacy and install page.

What is a mask?

A mask says where an effect or a layer shows. White shows it, black hides it, and grey is in between. You can paint one with a brush, draw a gradient, or select by brightness or colour.

Does Match colour get the match exactly?

It measures the average colour and spread of the subject and the background and moves one towards the other. That gives a close start. Fine-tune by eye.

Do depth and subject masks need anything extra?

A subject mask comes from the background remover. A depth mask needs a depth map: choose the Light tool and its Depth map button to make one on your device, after a one-off download of about 16 MB from this site.

Is the original photo kept?

Yes. Layers, masks and blend modes are settings in your design, so you can change or remove any of them.
