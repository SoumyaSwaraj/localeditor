What it does

  • Front and back as two pages, in eight styles, at 85 × 55 mm, 3.5 × 2 in, 90 × 54 mm, square or tall
  • 3 mm (0.125 in) bleed on every side with crop marks, and dashed trim and safe-area guides you can switch on to check
  • An optional QR code on the back that saves your contact details to a phone, or opens your website
  • Print sheets on A4 or US Letter with cut marks: a sheet of fronts and a sheet of backs that line up when printed on both sides
  • Exports: PNG or JPG, a PDF at the real size with bleed, or one card per row of a staff list

How it works

  1. Fill in your name, role, company and contact details, and add a logo or a photo if you have one.
  2. Choose a style and a card size, then tap Make it. The front and the back open in the editor as two pages.
  3. Download a PDF for a print shop, or add print sheets and print them yourself at 100 percent.

How do I print both sides so they line up?

Choose A4 or US Letter under Print sheets. You get a sheet of fronts and a sheet of backs with the cards in mirrored places. Print the fronts, put the paper back in and print the backs. The note at the top of the back sheet says which edge to flip on: the short edge when the sheet is wide, the long edge when it is tall. Print at 100 percent. Printers shift the paper a little, and the bleed gives room for that.

What are bleed and the safe area?

Bleed is 3 mm of background beyond the cut line (0.125 in for the 3.5 × 2 in size), so a cut that is slightly off leaves no white edge. The card page includes it: an 85 × 55 mm card is a 91 × 61 mm page. Text stays at least 5 mm inside the cut line.

How many cards fit on a sheet?

Nine 85 × 55 mm cards on A4 and eight 3.5 × 2 in cards on US Letter. That is fewer than on a pre-cut sheet, because each card keeps its bleed and the sheet keeps a margin for the printer.

Can I make cards for a whole team from a spreadsheet?

Yes. Tap Make one for each row of a list. Create many reads pasted cells or a CSV, TSV or Excel file and matches columns called name, role, phone and email. It fills the page you are looking at, so run it on the front, then on the back if the back carries a code. The QR code only changes per person when your list has a column for it, such as a profile link. Without one, every card carries the code from the form.

What does the QR code contain?

A standard contact card with the name, role, company, phone, email, website and address you typed, or only your website address. Nothing is looked up or sent anywhere. The code is drawn on your device.
