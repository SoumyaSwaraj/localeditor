What it does

  • 257 open-licence typefaces and 20 originals, loaded only when you use them
  • Ready-made text styles for titles and captions
  • Size, spacing, alignment, colour and shadow
  • Text stays editable after you save and reopen the design
  • Replace a font across the whole design in one step

How it works

  1. Open Text and pick a style or a plain text box.
  2. Type, then choose a typeface and colour.
  3. Drag it into place and download.

Your files stay on this device

Fonts are served from this site and saved for offline use, so no font service learns what you are writing.

Questions

Short, honest answers.

More on the privacy and install page.

Can I use my own font?

Not your own font files. The font library has 257 families and 20 originals, and your brand kit keeps the ones you use most.

Does text stay editable?

Yes. Save the design as a project and the text is still live text when you reopen it.

What if a font lacks a letter I typed?

The missing letters are drawn by a matching fallback typeface, and you are told once.
