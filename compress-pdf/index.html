Compress to a target size

Get a PDF under the upload limit.

Pick a target size and the PDF is tidied first, then photos and scans are made lighter step by step until it fits. Text and drawings stay sharp and selectable. If the target cannot be reached that way, you decide whether pages may become pictures.

Compress a PDF

FreeNo accountNothing uploaded

What it does

  • Targets: Email (10 MB), under 2 MB, under 500 KB, or a size you type
  • Tidies the file first, with no change in quality
  • Then makes photos lighter, one step at a time; text and drawings stay sharp
  • Several PDFs at once, as a zip
  • Optionally removes document details such as author and dates

How it works

  1. Open Bulk, then Compress to a size, and choose the PDFs.
  2. Pick a target size.
  3. Compress, and download the result.

Your files stay on this device

Compression runs on this device. Nothing is uploaded.

Questions

Short, honest answers.

More on the privacy and install page.

Will text stay sharp?

Yes, by default. Only photos are made lighter, and some images, such as CMYK and scans, are left exactly as they are.

What if it cannot reach the size?

It says which files could not reach it and offers to turn pages into pictures to get there. That makes the file smaller, but text can no longer be selected or searched, and links stop working.
