Recap collage

Turn a week, month or trip into one recap collage.

Pick four to thirty photos from a week, a month, a year or a trip. They go in the order they were taken when the photos carry a date, with a title, the dates and a line of your own stats.

What it does

  • Four to thirty photos in one design
  • Ordered by the date taken, or exactly as you picked them
  • Five styles: clean grid, big title, fitted rows, prints and contact sheet
  • A title, a date range suggested from your photos, and a line for your own stats
  • Square, 4:5 and story sizes, and every photo stays its own slot you can swap

How it works

  1. Open the maker and add your photos.
  2. Choose week, month, year or trip, then edit the title, dates and stats line.
  3. Pick a style and size, tap Make it, then download.

Your files stay on this device

What you type and the photos you add stay on this device. The preview and the finished design are drawn by your browser, and nothing is uploaded.

Questions

Short, honest answers.

More on the privacy and install page.

How does it know the dates?

From the date stored inside each photo, when there is one. Photos without a date go in the order you picked them, and you can type the dates yourself.

How many photos can I use?

Four to thirty. For fewer photos, use the collage maker.

Can I swap a photo afterwards?

Yes. Every photo is its own slot, so you can replace it, or move and resize it, in the studio.

Can I edit the result?

Yes. A maker produces ordinary layers. Tap any part to change it, move it or replace a photo, or use Edit details to reopen the form and remake the page.
