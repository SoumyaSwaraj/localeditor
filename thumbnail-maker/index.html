What it does

  • Eight layouts, each previewed with your photo and your headline
  • Cuts the subject out for you, or uses the whole photo
  • Bold headline fonts with a stroke or shadow so the words read when small
  • Built at 1280 × 720, a 16:9 shape
  • The result is ordinary layers you can keep editing

How it works

  1. Open the thumbnail maker and pick or upload a photo.
  2. Type a headline. Two to four words read best on a phone.
  3. Pick a layout. The thumbnail opens in the studio, ready to edit and download.

Your files stay on this device

Your photo and headline stay on this device. The cut-out runs here too, with a model that is downloaded once.

Questions

Short, honest answers.

More on the privacy and install page.

What size is a thumbnail?

1280 × 720 pixels, a 16:9 ratio.

Do I need to cut out the subject?

No. Choose Use the whole photo instead. A cut-out subject often stands out more at small sizes.

Why is it labelled Beta?

The cut-out uses the background remover, which works best on one clear subject.
