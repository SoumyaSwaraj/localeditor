What is a PDF watermark?

A PDF watermark is text drawn over each page to mark a document as confidential, a draft, a sample or owned by you. This tool draws the watermark as real vector text with the open-source pdf-lib library, using a semi-transparent fill so the content underneath stays readable.

You control the font (Helvetica, Times or Courier, regular or bold), size, opacity from 5% to 100%, rotation from −90° to 90° (45° is the classic diagonal), color, and a single centered stamp or a repeating tiled pattern across the whole page.

Everything runs locally in your browser; the PDF is never uploaded. Standard PDF fonts cover Latin characters (WinAnsi), so non-Latin scripts and emoji are not supported. A text watermark deters copying but can be removed by someone with a PDF editor.

Frequently asked questions

How do I add a watermark to a PDF for free?

Drop your PDF here, type the watermark text, adjust size, opacity and angle, then click Add watermark. The watermarked PDF downloads immediately with no signup.

Is the watermark added to every page?

Yes. The same watermark is drawn on all pages of the document, positioned relative to each page’s own size and orientation box.

What does tiled mean?

Tiled repeats the watermark text in a grid across the whole page, which makes it much harder to crop out than a single centered stamp.

Is my PDF uploaded to a server?

No. The watermark is applied with JavaScript on your device. Your file never leaves your browser, and the tool works offline.

Can I use any language or emoji?

The built-in standard PDF fonts support Latin characters only. Accented Western letters work, but scripts like Chinese, Arabic or emoji are not supported.

Can a watermark be removed?

A text watermark deters casual copying and clearly marks ownership, but a determined person with a PDF editor can remove it. For stronger protection, flatten or convert pages to images.
