What it does

  • Three layouts: product pages with photos, a price list, or a menu
  • Paste from a spreadsheet, or open a CSV or Excel file
  • Up to 300 rows, with name, price, description, category and a photo slot for each
  • A cover page, a contact line, your currency and an accent colour
  • Five styles, including an ink-saver one, on real paper sizes ready to print or save as PDF

How it works

  1. Open the maker and paste your rows, or open your CSV or Excel file.
  2. Choose product pages, a price list or a menu, then a style and a paper size.
  3. Tap Make it, fill in any photo slots, then download or print.

Your files stay on this device

What you type and the photos you add stay on this device. The preview and the finished design are drawn by your browser, and nothing is uploaded. A spreadsheet you open is read in your browser and is not uploaded.

Questions

Short, honest answers.

More on the privacy and install page.

What should my spreadsheet look like?

One product per row, with columns such as name, price, description, category, photo and code. You can also type rows into the form.

How many products fit?

Up to 300 rows in one catalogue. Pages are added as needed.

Can I add product photos?

Yes. Each product gets a photo slot that you can fill from your photos after the pages are made.

Does it sell anything or connect to a shop?

No. It only lays out pages. You print them or save them as a PDF.
