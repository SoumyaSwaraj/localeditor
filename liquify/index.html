Liquify and reshape

Push, pull and reshape with a brush.

Liquify moves pixels where you brush: tidy a curve, nudge an edge, bring a hem back into line. Face-aware liquify finds the face on your device and gives sliders for features such as eyes, nose, jaw and smile. Both are settings, so you can reset them.

What it does

  • Brush tools: forward warp, twirl, pucker, bloat, smooth and reconstruct
  • Freeze an area so the brush leaves it alone, and thaw it again
  • Face-aware sliders for face width, jaw, chin, eyes, nose, mouth and smile
  • Shape warp: bloat, pinch or squeeze a chosen oval area
  • Restore all puts everything back

How it works

  1. Open the studio in Expert mode, add a photo and choose the Liquify tool.
  2. Brush over the area, or for faces choose Face-aware liquify under Geometry, run Find faces and move the sliders.
  3. Check against the original in the before and after view, then download.

Your files stay on this device

Photos are edited by your browser on this device, using your graphics hardware. The face map for face-aware liquify comes from a small model that downloads from this site once, runs on your device and is listed in the storage manager. Nothing you edit is sent anywhere.

Questions

Short, honest answers.

More on the privacy and install page.

Can it be undone?

Yes. Strokes are kept as settings, so Restore all removes them, and undo steps back stroke by stroke.

Is there a limit to how far it moves things?

Yes. Displacement is capped at a share of the photo’s long side, which keeps results from tearing. Big changes need several small ones.

Does face-aware liquify work on every face?

Only on faces it finds. A small, turned or covered face can be missed, and a very strong slider setting looks unnatural, so use the before and after view.

Does it work on my phone?

It opens on a phone with a compact layout, but Expert mode is designed for a larger screen. The effects need WebGL 2, which most current phones and computers have; the Device check page shows what your browser supports.
