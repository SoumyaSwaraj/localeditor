Password-protect a PDF

Lock or unlock a PDF on this device.

Lock one or many PDFs so that opening them needs a password, or take a password off a file whose password you know. You choose what readers may do, such as printing and copying. The original file is not changed.

Protect a PDF

FreeNo accountNothing uploaded

What it does

  • AES-256 encryption, with AES-128 for older apps
  • Set what readers may do: printing, copying, changing, comments and forms. These are requests that some apps ignore
  • A password suggestion and strength meter
  • Lock or unlock several PDFs at once and get a zip
  • Remove a password you know

How it works

  1. Open Bulk, then Password-protect or unlock, and choose the PDFs.
  2. Type a password twice, or tap Suggest.
  3. Protect, then download the locked copy and test it once with the password.

Your files stay on this device

The file and the password stay on this device. Passwords are not stored or sent anywhere, and your original file is not changed.

Questions

Short, honest answers.

More on the privacy and install page.

What if I forget the password?

It cannot be recovered, not by LocalEditor and not by anyone else. Keep the password safe, and keep your original.

Can it remove a password I do not know?

No. To remove a password you have to type the current one.

Will every PDF app open the file?

AES-256 is the default and AES-128 is offered for older apps. Open the protected copy once with the password before you send it.
