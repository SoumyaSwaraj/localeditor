    What is an Image Converter?

    An image converter re-saves a picture in a different file format. This tool decodes any image your browser can open (JPG, PNG, WebP, GIF, BMP, SVG and, in most browsers, AVIF), draws it on a canvas and encodes it as JPG, PNG, WebP or a 24-bit BMP.

    PNG and WebP keep transparency; JPG and BMP do not, so transparent pixels are filled with the background color you choose (white by default). JPG and WebP use a quality setting from 10 to 100, while PNG and BMP are lossless. Animated GIFs are converted using their first frame.

    Files are processed locally on your device — nothing is uploaded, there is no sign-up, and the converter works offline.

    Frequently asked questions

    How do I convert PNG to JPG?

    Drop your PNG files, choose JPG as the output format, pick a background color for any transparent areas (white is the default), then download. The conversion runs instantly in your browser.

    How do I convert WebP to JPG or PNG?

    Add the WebP images and choose JPG or PNG. Choose PNG if the image has transparency you want to keep; choose JPG for the smallest widely compatible file.

    Why did the transparent background turn white?

    JPG and BMP do not support transparency, so transparent pixels must be filled with a solid color. Change the background color to any color you like, or convert to PNG or WebP to keep transparency.

    Is there a file size or number limit?

    There is no fixed limit. Images are converted one after another on your device, so the practical limit is your device memory. Very large images over about 100 megapixels may fail on phones.

    Is the converter safe for private photos?

    Yes. Conversion happens entirely in your browser and no file is ever uploaded. Re-encoding also strips EXIF metadata such as GPS location.
