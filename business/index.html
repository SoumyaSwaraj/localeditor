Questions

Short, honest answers.

More on the privacy and install page.

Can I keep my brand colours and fonts?

Yes. The brand kit stores your colours, fonts and logos in this browser, and they show up first whenever you pick a colour or font.

Can I make many product photos look the same?

Yes. Save an edit as a recipe and apply it to a batch of photos, then export them all at once.

Is it free for business use?

Yes. There is no paid tier and no watermark on exports.
