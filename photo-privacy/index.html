    1. Drop one or more photos onto the box, tap to choose them, or paste one.

    What can a photo carry?

    Phones and cameras write details into every photo: the place it was taken (often accurate to a few metres), the date, time and time zone, the make and model of the phone, sometimes its serial number or the owner’s name, and a small preview copy of the picture. Editing apps can add your name, captions, keywords, tagged faces and a list of the edits they made. Some phones attach a short video or a depth map after the picture.

    This page reads EXIF, GPS, XMP, IPTC, Photoshop, ICC, MPF and Content Credentials blocks in JPEG, the text, XMP and EXIF chunks in PNG, and the EXIF, XMP and ICC chunks in WebP. The clean copy leaves them out without decoding and re-saving the picture, so the image data is identical and nothing is lost. Its rotation is kept so it does not turn sideways, and the colour profile is kept unless you turn it off. The clean copy is read again before you download it, to check nothing was missed.

    HEIC and AVIF photos are read on a best-effort basis; this page cannot clean them without converting them. The studio’s exports never include any of these details.

    Frequently asked questions

    Is my photo uploaded anywhere?

    No. The photo is read and cleaned by this page inside your browser. Nothing is sent to a server, and the page keeps working with no connection once it has loaded.

    What can a photo reveal?

    Phones and cameras often store the exact place a photo was taken, the date and time and time zone, the make, model and sometimes serial number of the device, the app that edited it, and a small preview copy of the picture. Editing apps can add names, captions, keywords, tagged faces and an edit history.

    Does removing the details change the picture?

    No. For JPEG, PNG and WebP the clean copy keeps the picture data exactly as it was, byte for byte, so there is no quality loss. Only the hidden details around it are left out. The photo keeps its rotation so it does not turn sideways.

    Why does the hidden preview matter?

    Many photos carry a small preview copy. Some editing apps crop or edit the photo but leave the old preview in place, so it can still show what was cropped out. The clean copy removes it.

    Do social networks remove this anyway?

    Many large networks strip location when they publish a photo, but messaging apps sent as files, email, cloud links, forums and marketplaces often keep it. Cleaning the photo yourself means you do not have to rely on each service.

    Can it read iPhone HEIC photos?

    It reads the details in HEIC photos on a best-effort basis, but it cannot remove them without converting the photo. On iPhone you can turn off location for a single share with Options, then Location, in the share sheet, or set Camera, Formats to Most Compatible.
