What it does

  • Two to twenty slides, and you can add or remove slides as you go
  • Dashed guides mark the slide edges, and layers snap to them
  • Photos and text can cross from one slide to the next
  • Start a new Instagram carousel at 1080 × 1350 per slide
  • A swipe preview, then numbered slides in a zip as JPG or PNG

How it works

  1. Open Download, choose Slides, and pick how many slides.
  2. Design across the dashed slide guides.
  3. Check the swipe preview, then download the numbered slides and post them in order.

Questions

Short, honest answers.

More on the privacy and install page.

How many slides can I make?

Two to twenty. Choose the count when you start, then add or remove slides in the Slides panel.

Which sizes work?

Start a new Instagram carousel at 1080 × 1350 per slide, or turn any page you already have into slides.

Will the seams line up?

Use the swipe preview to check before you post. On a phone, a very wide carousel is drawn in pieces, so a photo or gradient background can show a faint seam.
