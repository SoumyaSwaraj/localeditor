Themes and appearance

Choose how it looks and feels.

Pick a theme and a mode and the whole site follows: pages, tools and both studios. The choice is remembered on this device only.

FreeNo accountNothing uploaded

What it does

  • Clear, the default: calm greys and one blue
  • Lapis: the earlier blue and apricot design
  • Graphite: monochrome, compact and square, for people who live in professional tools
  • Paper: warm and quiet, easy on the eyes for long PDF work
  • Contrast: black and white, thicker focus rings, larger text and larger targets

How it works

  1. Choose Open Appearance, or Appearance in the menu on any page.
  2. Pick a theme card, and light, dark or automatic.
  3. The change applies at once and is remembered on this device.

Your files stay on this device

Your choice is stored in this browser only. It is not sent anywhere and is not tied to an account, because there is none.

Questions

Short, honest answers.

More on the privacy and install page.

Does it change my designs or exports?

No. A theme only changes how the app looks. The editing canvas keeps neutral surroundings, so colours in your picture are not affected.

Will my choice follow me to another device?

No. It is stored in the browser you chose it in.

Is there a theme for people who need more contrast?

Yes, Contrast. If your device asks for more contrast, the app suggests it but does not switch by itself.
