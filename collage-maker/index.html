Make a photo collage, every cell its own photo.

What it does

  • Layouts sorted by how many photos you have
  • Styles such as instant-print wall, film strip, scrapbook and magazine
  • Every cell is a separate photo: drag to swap, pan and zoom inside it
  • Spacing, corner radius, border and background colour
  • Empty cells you can fill later, from your photos or a stock search you start

How it works

  1. Open the collage maker and choose a layout or a style.
  2. Add your photos. They fill the cells in order, and you can drag to swap them.
  3. Adjust spacing and corners, add text if you like, then download.

Your files stay on this device

Your photos are placed and saved by the browser on this device. They are not uploaded, and the collage is exported without a watermark.

Questions

Short, honest answers.

More on the privacy and install page.

How many photos can a collage have?

Layouts go from two photos to large grids. Pick one by photo count, or start from a style and add or remove cells.

Can I change a photo after placing it?

Yes. Select a cell and replace its photo; the crop, shape and border of the cell stay the same.

What size is the finished collage?

Any size the studio offers, including square, portrait and story sizes for social posts, and print sizes.
